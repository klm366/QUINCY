**ICON-Land** is a framework jointly developed by the Max-Planck Institutes for Meteorology [(MPI-M)](https://www.mpimet.mpg.de) and Biogeochemistry [(MPI-B)](https://www.bgc-jena.mpg.de) for the modelling of land processes in ICON. The framrework can be used as a stand-alone land-surface model (Nabel et al., 2020), as land-atmosphere model in the ICON-A atmosphere-only model (Schneck et al., 2022) and in the fully coupled ICON-ESM Earth System Model (Jungclaus et al., 2021).

**ICON-Land** has been designed in a modular way that separates infrastructure from science to allow for the integration of concurrent and alternative process and surface descriptions in a flexible and easy-to-use way. Specific process implementations include the [JSBACHv4](page/2-science-guide/01-jsbach/index.html) and the [QUINCY](page/2-science-guide/02-quincy/index.html) model configurations.
