**ICON-Land** is the framework for the modelling of land processes in the general circulation model [ICON](https://www.icon-model.org).
It is jointly developed by the Max-Planck Institutes for Meteorology [(MPI-M)](https://www.mpimet.mpg.de) and Biogeochemistry [(MPI-B)](https://www.bgc-jena.mpg.de).

**ICON-Land** can be used as a stand-alone land-surface model (Nabel et al., 2020), as well as coupled to the ICON model
 in various configurations (Jungclaus et al., 2021, Schneck et al., 2022, Müller et al. 2025).

**ICON-Land** has been designed in a modular way that separates infrastructure from science to allow for the integration of concurrent
and alternative process and surface descriptions in a flexible and easy-to-use way. Specific process implementations include the
[JSBACH](guide2/2-science-guide/01-jsbach/index.html) and [QUINCY](guide2/2-science-guide/02-quincy/index.html) model configurations.
Note that TRRA is currently not included in ICON-Land.
