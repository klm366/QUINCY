\section{Land Model Interface}


The interface (drivers/mo{\_}land{\_}model{\_}interface.f90) runs SLM for one time step.
\\

\textbf{Processes are:}
\begin{easylist}[itemize]
& radiation (1 RAD)
& photosynthesis (named assimilation in JSBACH4) (2 PHOT)
& vegetation (3 VEG)
& soil (4 SOIL)
\end{easylist}
~\\
Note, the names (short and long) and process numbers (currently 1-4) may be changed in the future to be consistent with jsbach4.
~\\

\textbf{Call-Tree a.k.a. Queue:}
\\
\begin{easylist}[enumerate]
& update{\_}radiation() RAD
& update{\_}vegsoil{\_}bgc SOIL
& update{\_}phenology VEG
& update{\_}canopy{\_}fluxes PHOT
& calc{\_}growth VEG
& calc{\_}turnover VEG
& update{\_}veg{\_}dynamics VEG
& update{\_}soil{\_}pool SOIL
& update{\_}vegsoil{\_}physics SOIL
& update{\_}veg{\_}pools VEG
\end{easylist}
