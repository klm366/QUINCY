CHELSA climate inputs for PFT13 trait regressions

Files:
raw_climate/CHELSA_bio01_1981-2010_V.2.1.tif
raw_climate/CHELSA_bio12_1981-2010_V.2.1.tif

BIO01:
Mean annual near-surface air temperature.
Stored as Kelvin x 10.
Convert to Celsius as:
temperature_C = bio01 / 10.0 - 273.15

BIO12:
Annual precipitation.
Stored as mm/year.

These files are used only as climate predictors for TRY trait-climate regressions.
