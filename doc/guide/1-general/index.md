### ICON-Land Research Groups

**MPI-M**:  Land-climate interactions, esp. in high latitudes

**MPI-B**: The [Terrestrial Biosphere Group](https://www.bgc-jena.mpg.de/bsi) at the Max Planck Institute for Biogeochemistry focusses on an improved represetation of ecophysiological processes in ICON-Land, especially the coupling of biogeochemical processes and their effects of land-atmosphere feedback mechanims, including biogeophysical and biogeochemical feedback mechanisms. The group has developed the QUINCY configuration of ICON-Land. 

**DWD**: Land-Atmosphere Coupling, TERRA

**LMU**: Land-use and land management, climate-carbon cycle interactions
